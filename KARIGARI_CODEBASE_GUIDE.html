01 · What is KARIGARI?

A super-honest helper for village artisans

Imagine someone who weaves beautiful sarees by hand. They're brilliant at making things, but they face three big problems. KARIGARI is built to fix all three.

😟 Middlemen cheat them

A trader buys the saree cheap and resells it for a lot, keeping the profit.

Fix: KARIGARI connects the maker straight to the buyer, and pays them ~89 of every ₹100.

😕 Selling online is hard

They may not read English, type well, or have good internet.

Fix: They just talk to the app; a robot cleans the photo and writes the description. It even works offline, by SMS, or by phone call.

😠 Fakes copy them

Cheap machine-made copies pretend to be real handloom.

Fix: A QR sticker + a live-photo AI check proves each item is 100% real.

In one line: KARIGARI is a "trust bridge" — it links the maker to the buyer, proves the craft is real, and makes sure the maker is paid fairly. Its motto: "Fair pay. Proven craft. Every time."

The notebook's design lives in prisma/schema.prisma. It has 13 pages (tables). Here's what each one remembers.

The star of the show: a CraftItem's "status"

A product moves through life-stages like a caterpillar becoming a butterfly. The most important field on it is its status:

PENDING_VERIFICATION  # maker just uploaded it; waiting for an officer
        ↓
VERIFIED              # officer approved it + printed a unique QR tag
        ↓
TAG_ATTACHED          # maker stuck the QR on + re-photographed it
        ↓
ADVANCE_PAID          # maker chose how to sell (+ got money early!)
        ↓
SOLD_FINAL            # buyer scanned QR, AI confirmed real, sale done
        ↓
PAYOUT_COMPLETED      # the money was settled

# ⚠ side path: if a buyer's scan keeps failing → FLAGGED → APPLIED_FOR_REVIEW
The clever bit: every time an item changes status, the app writes a line in the AuditLog diary. Nothing can be secretly changed, so the government can trust the whole story of every item.

06 · Who uses it

The three kinds of people

There are only two login roles in the code (ADMIN and ARTISAN), but three kinds of people use the app.

🧵 The Artisan

The maker. Logs in, uploads crafts, gets paid. Most of the app is built for them.

🏛️ The Admin

The government helper — one login, two views: Facilitator (field worker: checks items, prints QR tags, sees real names) and Nodal (big-boss: watches the numbers + diary, never sees private info).

🛍️ The Buyer

Doesn't even need an account! They just scan a QR or shop the public store. Their name ties their orders together.

07 · The big features & where they live

Each feature — what, where, how

The hackathon asked for three AI features. KARIGARI built those and much more. Chips are colour-coded: brain waiter screen.

🎙️ Talk instead of type

What

The maker speaks in Odia/Hindi/Telugu/English. The app writes it down, translates it, figures out the product, the days of work, and material cost — and asks a follow-up if something's missing.

Wheresrc/lib/voiceParse.tsapi/items/voice-parseapi/ivr/collect-item
How

Audio → Groq Whisper (fast). Odia isn't supported there, so it falls back to Gemini, which handles it. If both robots are down, her exact spoken words are still saved.

📸 Make photos look professional

What

Turns a messy phone photo into a clean, white-background catalogue shot.

Wheresrc/lib/imageEnhance.ts
How

A machine-learning "magic eraser" runs on the phone (private, no internet), cuts out the product, adds a soft shadow, fixes light & color, sharpens. Too-weak phone? It skips the cut-out instead of freezing.

💰 A fair price, both ways

What

Suggests a fair price and warns if it's too low (a middleman squeezing) OR too high (gouging the buyer).

Wheresrc/lib/pricing.tssrc/lib/benchmarkData.ts
How

Fair floor = (days × daily wage) + materials + 10%. Market price = 1.2×–1.6× that. A wild price gets flagged for a human. Impossible claims ("saree took 200 days") are rejected.

🔒 Prove it's real (QR + AI Vision)

What

Each real item gets a physical QR sticker. To buy, you take a live photo and the AI checks it matches the original.

Whereapi/admin/verify-batchapi/verify-authenticitycomponents/VerificationCamera.tsx
How

Gemini returns a similarity score. ≥75 = real → sold. There's a 5-minute, 10-try grace window so a bad angle doesn't fail an honest buyer. Keeps failing → flagged, maker's health score drops 15 (a real match later restores it).

🤝 Fair money, no middleman

What

Of every ₹100 a buyer pays, ~₹89 goes straight to the maker (40% on dispatch, ~49% on delivery). A promoter, if any, gets ₹5 from the platform's share — never the maker's.

Wheresrc/lib/escrow.tssrc/lib/razorpay.tsapi/payments/*
How

Razorpay only collects money, so the final pay-out is a recorded "programmatic settlement". The ledger, math & diary are real; the bank transfer is simulated in this prototype — and the code never pretends otherwise.

📶 Works with no internet

What

The app installs like a real app and keeps working offline. Captures made offline upload themselves when signal returns.

Wherenext.config.tssrc/lib/offlineQueue.tssrc/lib/offlineSync.ts
How

A "service worker" caches the app. New captures wait in the phone's IndexedDB and are replayed automatically when back online. A row is deleted only after the server confirms it saved — nothing is ever lost.

📱 Reach people with no smartphone

What

Basic-phone users get an SMS when a buyer wants their craft (reply "1" to accept), or can call a toll-free number and just talk.

Wheresrc/lib/sms.tssrc/lib/twilioIvr.tsapi/ivr/*api/sms/inbound
How

Twilio sends the SMS + runs the phone menu. A call's audio uses the same voiceParse.ts brain as the app, so the phone and app always agree. Phone webhooks are signature-checked so nobody can fake a call.

🏛️ Government schemes made easy

What

Tells a maker which government help-schemes they qualify for (PM Vishwakarma, NSFDC, GeM…) and helps them apply.

Wheresrc/lib/schemes.tssrc/lib/artisanEligibility.ts/artisan/schemes
How

Pure, readable rules — no AI black box — so the answer is auditable. Anything that can't be checked from data becomes a tick-box the maker declares, never silently auto-passed.

🛒 One listing, many shops

What

A single listing can broadcast to many places (Paytm & Magicpin via ONDC, GeM, Amazon Karigar) — without opening any seller accounts.

Wheresrc/lib/syndication.tssrc/lib/ondcCatalog.tssrc/lib/gemCatalog.ts
How

Generates the correct data file for each platform + a price comparison proving "buy direct = cheapest". It marks items broadcast-ready; it doesn't literally push to live marketplaces in the prototype — and says so.

👥 Neighbours helping neighbours

What

Weavers team up to fill big orders (cluster pooling), lend materials, and answer bulk buyer demands. Plus a creator/influencer program.

Wheresrc/lib/notifications.tssrc/lib/creators.tsapi/demand/*/artisan/cluster
How

When a buyer posts a demand, the app scores which makers match best (a "Sambalpuri" weaver ranks above a generic "silk" one) and alerts them in-app + by SMS.

08 · src/lib

The brains

src/lib is where the clever thinking lives, kept separate from the screens so it can be reused and tested. The big ones to know:

pricing.ts

The fair-price engine: fair-wage floor + market band, and the "too low / too high" guard. One source of truth for every screen.

escrow.ts

The money-splitting rules — exactly how ₹100 is divided between maker, platform, and (maybe) a creator.

gemini.ts & groq.ts

The connections to the robot helpers. Both cleverly try several AI models in order, and tell "busy, retry" apart from "wrong password".

voiceParse.ts

Turns spoken words (4 languages) into neat product fields. Shared by the app AND the phone line, so they never disagree.

imageEnhance.ts

The photo magic-eraser + beautifier that runs right on the phone.

schemes.ts

The government-scheme rulebook — pure rules, no AI, fully auditable.

offlineQueue.ts + offlineSync.ts

The offline safety net: save captures on the phone, auto-upload when internet returns.

auditLogger.ts

The tiny helper that writes a line in the unerasable diary every time something important happens.

Smaller helpers (grouped): prisma.ts (opens the notebook), artisanAuth.ts (the bouncer), sms.ts/twilioIvr.ts (texting + phone line), razorpay*.ts (payments), syndication.ts/ondcCatalog.ts/gemCatalog.ts (marketplace files), notifications.ts (demand matching), festivals.ts/suppliers.ts/giLabels.ts/indiaGeo.ts (data lists), translations.ts + i18n/ (4 languages), and small utilities like utils.ts and urlTab.ts.

10 · src/app pages

The screens

Any file called page.tsx is a screen; a loading.tsx beside it is the "please wait" placeholder; a layout.tsx is a frame wrapped around a group of screens.

  • layout.tsx (top) — the outer frame: loads fonts, makes the app installable, and mounts the offline auto-uploader once for everything.

  • offline/ — the friendly page shown with no internet.

12 · How it's written

Two golden patterns that repeat everywhere

Remember just these two things about how this code is written — they're what make it trustworthy, and judges love them.

🛟 Pattern A — "The robot always has a backup"

Every robot/helper can fail (no internet, wrong key, too busy). The code never crashes; it degrades gracefully:

  • Gemini busy? → try the next AI model.
  • Whisper can't do Odia? → fall back to Gemini for the audio.
  • All robots down? → keep the maker's exact words + safe placeholder numbers, clearly marked as placeholders.
  • Twilio/Razorpay not set up? → the demand still posts / an honest "checkout not configured" appears.

🫡 Pattern B — "Be honest about real vs pretend"

It's a prototype, and the code is refreshingly honest about it — it never dresses a simulation up as the real thing:

  • The final bank pay-out is a recorded settlement, never labelled a confirmed bank transfer.
  • The demo charge is ₹1 on purpose, while every displayed price stays real.
  • Marketplace "syndication" makes the correct files but doesn't literally push to Amazon/Paytm yet — the comments say so.
  • The AuditLog diary, escrow ladder, pricing math, QR checks, offline queue and login are all genuinely real.
